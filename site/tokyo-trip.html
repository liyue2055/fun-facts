🏨 Hotels

1 · Oct 13–18 — JR East Hotel Mets Premier Akihabara

Right by Akihabara Station. Electric town, arcades, anime — and one stop from Kanda-Jimbocho's book town. Check-in 3:00pm, check-out 11:00am.

2 · Oct 18–27 — Hotel Bokusyusou Tokyo

Sumida-ku (2-29-14 Higashi-Mukojima), 1 min from Hikifune Station, ~15 min walk to Tokyo Skytree and the Sumida River, near Asakusa. Aparthotel with kitchen. Meeting days Oct 19–22 fall here. Check-in 3:00pm, check-out 10:00am (earlier than the others). No front desk — check-in instructions arrive by email; no elevator.

3 · Oct 27–Nov 2 — JR-East Hotel Mets Shibuya

Minutes from Shibuya Station. Check-in 3:00pm, check-out 11:00am. On Nov 2 the flight is 5:10pm — ask the hotel to hold your bags after check-out.

👥 Team outings team

Akihabara (Oct 13–18)

  • Street Kart go-kart tour — costume karting through Akiba, ~10 min walk from the station. ~¥10,000/person, English guide, fuel & costume included. Book ahead online.
  • Game Boy mod workshop — build your own modded Game Boy / GBA with staff guidance, take it home. Welcomes corporate groups, English-speaking.
  • Ninja wire-action experience — stunt-wire "flying" with a pro action crew, edited video included. Group-friendly.
  • Group dinner: Yamato Akihabara — 1 min from the station, 173 seats, private rooms, banquet courses ¥3,500–6,000 with all-you-can-drink. Alt: Torarembou (BiTO AKIBA PLAZA 9F) — private rooms for 10–20, English menu, night views.
⚠️ Action needed before flying: Street Kart requires every driver to hold a 1949 Geneva Convention International Driving Permit — arrange at home, it can't be done in Japan.

Sumida / Asakusa (Oct 18–27) — evenings, meeting days Oct 19–22

  • ⭐ Yakatabune dinner cruise — traditional roofed houseboat on the Sumida River, departs Asakusa (~25 min from hotel). 2.5–3 hours, full Japanese course (sashimi boat, tempura…) + free-flow drinks. ¥15,000–18,000/person, English OK. The single most memorable group night. Free cancellation up to 10 days out — book now.
  • Asakusa Sumo Club dinner show — 18:00 & 20:30 seatings. Retired pro wrestlers demo + bouts, geisha dance opener, chanko hot-pot meal; audience can step into the dohyo.
  • WASAKE sake tasting (Asakusa) — 105-min "Deep Dive": 10 tastings incl. Dassai vertical flight. ¥8,000/person, fluent-English hosts, max 10/group — split larger teams.
  • Sushi Making Tokyo (Asakusa) — hands-on nigiri & maki class. English + 简体中文 / 繁體中文 — the best bilingual fit on this list.
  • Kabuki single-act tickets (Kabukiza, Ginza) — watch just one act (30 min–2 hrs), ¥1,500–4,000, with English and simplified-Chinese caption devices. ~30 min from Asakusa. Evening shows available.
🌅 Morning option before meetings: sumo stable practice (asageiko) at Takasago stable, Ryogoku (~15 min away). Meet 07:45, 90–120 min, photos with wrestlers after. ~¥13,500/person, max 20/group, English guide. Sells out — book as soon as dates are fixed.

Shibuya (Oct 27–Nov 2)

  • Shibuya Sky — 47th-floor open-air deck at sunset. ¥2,700–3,400/person. Tickets drop exactly 14 days ahead at midnight JST and sunset slots sell out in minutes — assign someone to grab them at release.
  • Karaoke night — Big Echo / Karaoke Kan / Manekineko, minutes from the station. Private rooms, English song menus, ~¥300–500 per 30-min block. The no-fail team night.
  • Group dinner: DOMA-DOMA Shibuya — 2 min from the station, private rooms, English menu, party plans from ~¥2,500.
  • Omakase sushi-making class (hidden bar, Shibuya) — 1.5 hrs, max 5 people. Better for a small group than the whole team.

👥 团队活动 团队

秋叶原（10月13–18日）

  • 街头卡丁车——穿玩偶服开卡丁车穿过秋叶原，车站步行约10分钟。约¥10,000/人，英文向导，含服装油费。提前在线预订。
  • Game Boy 改装工坊——亲手改装一台 Game Boy/GBA 并带回家，接待企业团建，英文服务。
  • 忍者威亚体验——专业动作团队指导"飞檐走壁"，含剪辑视频，适合组团。
  • 团队聚餐：大和秋叶原店——车站1分钟，173席，有包间，宴会套餐¥3,500–6,000含畅饮。备选：Torarembou（BiTO AKIBA PLAZA 9楼）——10–20人包间，英文菜单，夜景。
⚠️ 出发前必须办：卡丁车要求每位驾驶员持有1949年《日内瓦公约》国际驾照——必须在国内办好，到日本办不了。

墨田 / 浅草（10月18–27日）——会议日（10月19–22日）主攻晚上

  • ⭐ 屋形船晚宴游船——隅田川上的传统屋形船，从浅草出发（酒店过去约25分钟）。2.5–3小时，日式全席（刺身船、生鱼片、天妇罗……）+ 酒水畅饮。¥15,000–18,000/人，英文可。全程最难忘的团建之夜。10天前免费取消——现在就订。
  • 浅草相扑晚宴秀——18:00 / 20:30 两场。退役力士表演+三番赛、艺伎开场舞、相扑火锅餐；观众可上土俵体验。
  • WASAKE 清酒品鉴（浅草）——105分钟深度品鉴：10款含獺祭垂直品鉴。¥8,000/人，英文主持，每团最多10人——大团队可分两批。
  • 寿司制作体验 Tokyo（浅草）——手握寿司+卷物实操。英文 + 简体中文 / 繁體中文——本清单里双语适配最佳。
  • 歌舞伎单幕票（银座歌舞伎座）——只看一幕（30分钟–2小时），¥1,500–4,000，提供英文和简体中文字幕机。浅草过去约30分钟，有夜场。
🌅 会议日早晨备选：两国高砂部屋相扑晨训参观（距酒店约15分钟）。7:45集合，90–120分钟，结束后可与力士合影。约¥13,500/人，每团最多20人，英文向导。经常售罄——日期一定马上订。

涩谷（10月27日–11月2日）

  • Shibuya Sky——47楼露天观景台看日落。¥2,700–3,400/人。门票提前14天日本时间午夜开售，日落时段几分钟售罄——派专人卡点抢票。
  • 卡拉OK之夜——Big Echo / Karaoke馆 / Manekineko，车站几分钟。包间、英文歌单，约¥300–500/30分钟。零失败的团建夜。
  • 团队聚餐：土间土间涩谷店——车站2分钟，包间，英文菜单，聚餐套餐约¥2,500起。
  • Omakase 寿司制作课（涩谷隐秘酒吧）——1.5小时，最多5人。更适合小分队而非全团。

☕ Coffee map solo

Near Akihabara

  • Kanda-Jimbocho kissaten crawl — one stop south, Tokyo's book town. Showa-era coffee houses along Yasukuni-dori: hand-dripped coffee, thick toast, firm caramel pudding. Just wander.
  • Café de l'Ambre, Ginza (~20 min) — pouring since 1948, famous for aged beans (some decades old). English menu. The connoisseur pilgrimage.
  • Kayaba Coffee, Yanaka — a 1916 folk house turned kissaten in old-town Yanaka. Pair with a Yanaka-Ginza stroll.

Near Sumida

  • Kiyosumi-Shirakawa roaster crawl (~25 min) — Tokyo's third-wave ground zero: Cream of the Crop (the original warehouse roaster), Arise Coffee Roasters (grungy, great beans to take home), Allpress Espresso roastery, and Fukadaso Cafe — a modern kissaten in a preserved 50-year-old apartment building, famous for its pudding. Weekday mornings are quietest.
  • Royal Coffee, Asakusa — old-school kissaten near Senso-ji. Pair with an early Asakusa walk before the crowds.

Near Shibuya

  • Fuglen, Tomigaya — Norwegian roaster by Yoyogi Park. Grab a cup and walk the park to Meiji Shrine early morning.
  • Bear Pond Espresso, Shimokitazawa — cult spot. Order the "Dirty." Pair with a Shimokita vintage trawl.
  • Chatei Hatou, Shibuya — a kissaten legend, dead serious about its coffee. Quiet refuge minutes from the scramble.

☕ 咖啡地图 独行

秋叶原附近

  • 神保町喫茶店巡礼——南边一站，东京书街。靖国通两旁的昭和风咖啡馆：手冲咖啡、厚吐司、硬布丁。不用规划，随便逛。
  • Café de l'Ambre，银座（约20分钟）——1948年开业至今，以陈年咖啡豆闻名（有些存放数十年）。有英文菜单。咖啡发烧友的朝圣地。
  • Kayaba Coffee，谷中——1916年民居改建的喫茶店，谷中老街区。可搭配谷中银座散步。

墨田附近

  • 清澄白河烘焙巡礼（约25分钟）——东京第三波咖啡发源地：Cream of the Crop（最早的仓库烘焙坊）、Arise Coffee Roasters（grunge风，豆子值得带走）、Allpress Espresso烘焙坊，以及深田荘 Café——50年老公寓改建的现代喫茶店，布丁出名。工作日早晨最安静。
  • Royal Coffee，浅草——浅草寺附近的老派喫茶店。适合赶在人群前来个清晨浅草散步。

涩谷附近

  • Fuglen，富谷——代代木公园旁的挪威烘焙商。买一杯，穿过公园去明治神宫看清晨。
  • Bear Pond Espresso，下北泽——传奇小店。点一杯 "Dirty"。搭配下北泽古着淘宝。
  • 茶亭羽當，涩谷——喫茶店传奇，对咖啡极其认真。涩谷喧嚣中几分钟之外的安静避难所。

🤝 1-on-1 ideas pairs

For an evening with one friend or coworker:

  • Golden Gai bar hop, Shinjuku — 200 closet-sized bars seating 5–8. Built for pairs; do 2–3 bars in a night. Cover charges (~¥1,000) are normal.
  • Jazz kissa evening — dim listening bars where the music is the point. Eagle in Yotsuya (est. 1967) is the famous one — note: strictly silent, no conversation until 6pm, then it loosens into a bar where talking is fine. Go in the evening.
  • Tea ceremony for two — private sessions in Asakusa, 45–60 min, English available. Book a day or two ahead.
  • Coffee crawl for two — take someone through Kiyosumi-Shirakawa. The roasters are tiny and the whole loop is walkable.
  • Harmonica Yokocho, Kichijoji — lantern-lit alley of tiny izakayas. Made for hopping with one other person; half the fun is squeezing into a 6-seat counter.
  • Pottery / kintsugi workshop for two — Asakusa has English-friendly studios, 1–1.5 hrs. Book ahead.
One perfect evening: kissaten coffee at Kayaba in Yanaka at golden hour → wander Nezu Shrine → backstreet dinner hopping. Very Tokyo, zero tourist-bus energy.

Solo decompression

  • Daikoku-yu sento (Sumida, ~15–20 min from hotel 2) — tattoo-friendly public bathhouse, natural hot-spring baths, rooftop deck with Skytree views and hammocks. ~¥550. Perfect after meeting days; no booking needed.
  • Shimokitazawa (5 min by train from Shibuya) — vintage quarter: secondhand clothing, records, old cameras. The walk is the experience.

🤝 双人活动灵感 双人

和朋友或同事度过一个晚上：

  • 黄金街酒吧巡游，新宿——200家袖珍酒吧，每家5–8席。天生为双人设计，一晚逛2–3家。座位费（约¥1,000）是常态。
  • 爵士喫茶之夜——昏暗的聆听酒吧，音乐本身就是主角。四谷的 Eagle（1967年开业）最有名——注意：18点前严格肃静禁语，之后转为可交谈的酒吧模式。请晚上去。
  • 双人茶道体验——浅草私人场，45–60分钟，有英文。提前1–2天预订。
  • 双人咖啡巡礼——带同事逛清澄白河。烘焙坊都很小，整条路线可步行。
  • Harmonica 横丁，吉祥寺——灯笼点亮的居酒屋小巷。为双人 hopping 而生；挤进6人小柜台本身就是一半乐趣。
  • 双人陶艺 / 金缮工坊——浅草有英文友好的工作室，1–1.5小时。提前预订。
完美的一晚：黄昏时分在谷中 Kayaba 喝喫茶咖啡 → 漫步根津神社 → 穿小巷吃晚餐。非常东京，零游客大巴气息。

独处放空

  • 大黑汤钱汤（墨田区，距2号酒店约15–20分钟）——纹身友好大众浴场，天然温泉，屋顶露台可看晴空塔+吊床。约¥550。会议日后完美解压，无需预订。
  • 下北泽（涩谷坐车5分钟）——古着区：二手服装、黑胶、旧相机、手工杂货。散步本身就是体验。
