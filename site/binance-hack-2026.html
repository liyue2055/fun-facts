The Binance Hack of October 29, 2026 — What We Know So Far

2026年10月29日币安被黑事件 — 目前已知的情况（持续更新中）

Status / 状态： Updated Nov 7, 2026 (one-week check-back; checked Nov 6 20:10 EST / Nov 7 01:10 UTC). Binance published an initial findings report on Nov 3, which materially rewrote the attack story (see §3), but its full technical post-mortem is still pending — Binance itself calls the Nov 3 report initial/preliminary, says the investigation remains ongoing, findings will face independent review, and some technical details will remain confidential. Secondary outlets calling the Nov 3 report “the post-mortem” do not change that. Recovery has advanced: ~98% replenished, Proof of Reserves expanded to 44 tokens, and withdrawals reopening in phases through Recovery Update #9 (Nov 6).
2026 年 11 月 7 日更新（一周回访核查；核查时间：美东 11 月 6 日 20:10 / UTC 11 月 7 日 01:10）。币安已于 11 月 3 日发布初步调查报告，该报告实质性改写了攻击经过（见第 3 节），但完整技术复盘仍未发布——币安自己称 11 月 3 日报告为初步报告，表示调查仍在进行、结论将接受独立审查，且部分技术细节将保密。二手媒体将 11 月 3 日报告称为“复盘”并不改变这一点。恢复工作已推进：已补回约 98%，储备金证明扩大至 44 种代币，提现已通过第 9 号恢复更新（11 月 6 日）分阶段恢复中。

TL;DR

On Thu Oct 29, 2026, attackers drained Binance's hot wallets — the end of a months-long, multi-stage campaign, not a one-off laptop compromise. Binance's Nov 3 initial report puts affected assets at ~$6.34 billion — the largest crypto theft on record, about 4× the 2025 Bybit heist — with ~$2.85 billion frozen or intercepted (subject to reconciliation), 98% of affected tokens replenished as of Oct 31, more than 100% of the needed amount set aside from Binance's own resources, and users bearing no loss. The campaign ran: a tampered AI coding-tool installer (June), a fake job-interview malware “assignment” (September), abuse of an internal AI tool to pivot to wallet engineers (Oct 20), then unauthorized wallet-signing access and EIP-7702 delegations (Oct 29). No wallet private keys leaked — the signing system itself was misused, and pausing withdrawals could not stop the on-chain delegations. Binance cites the FBI attributing the attack to North Korea (DPRK); FBI/TRM/Chainalysis reporting names the TraderTraitor unit, and North Korea has denied it. AI angle — revised: attackers abused AI tooling and the access it carried; there is still no evidence an autonomous AI agent caused the theft (see §5).

2026 年 10 月 29 日（周四），攻击者掏空了币安热钱包——这是一场历时数月的多阶段攻击的终点，而非单一一台笔记本被攻陷。币安 11 月 3 日初步报告称受影响资产约 63.4 亿美元，为史上最大加密货币盗窃案，约为 2025 年 Bybit 事件的 4 倍；约 28.5 亿美元已被冻结或拦截（待对账确认），截至 10 月 31 日受影响代币已补回 98%，币安已从自有资源中拨备超过所需金额的 100%，用户不承担损失。攻击链为：被篡改的 AI 编程工具安装包（6 月）、假招聘面试中的恶意“作业”（9 月）、滥用内部 AI 工具横向渗透至钱包工程师（10 月 20 日），以及未授权的钱包签名访问与 EIP-7702 委托（10 月 29 日）。钱包私钥并未泄露——被滥用的是签名系统本身，暂停提现也无法阻止链上委托。币安援引 FBI 将攻击归因于朝鲜；FBI/TRM/Chainalysis 的报道点名 TraderTraitor 组织，朝鲜已否认。AI 角度——已修正：攻击者滥用了 AI 工具及其携带的权限；目前仍没有证据表明自主 AI 智能体实施了此次盗窃（见第 5 节）。

3. How it happened 攻击手法

Binance's Nov 3 initial report replaces the preliminary “one compromised laptop” account with a complex, multi-stage campaign (timeline in §1): a supply-chain-style entry via a tampered AI coding-tool installer in June — detected at the time, with the device replaced and credentials rotated, but with the engineer's role and colleagues already exposed; a September fake-recruitment intrusion whose malware was not detected by endpoint tools at the time; an Oct 20 pivot in which an internal AI tool was used from the compromised machine to reach wallet engineers and a container-deployment permission; and the Oct 29 drain via unauthorized access to the internal wallet signing service and EIP-7702 delegations. Binance stresses no wallet private-key leak: this was signing-service misuse, not key theft. Binance also says it blocked 57 suspected North Korea-linked threats in 2026 before this campaign got through.

币安 11 月 3 日初步报告已取代初步的“一台笔记本被攻陷”说法，确认这是一场复杂的多阶段攻击（时间线见第 1 节）：6 月以被篡改的 AI 编程工具安装包作为供应链式入口——当时虽被发现、已更换设备并轮换凭证，但工程师的岗位与同事信息已经暴露；9 月通过假招聘入侵，其恶意代码当时未被终端工具检出；10 月 20 日从被攻陷机器出发，滥用内部 AI 工具横向触达钱包工程师与容器部署权限；10 月 29 日通过未授权访问内部钱包签名服务与 EIP-7702 委托完成盗转。币安强调钱包私钥并未泄露：这是签名服务被滥用，而非密钥被盗。币安还表示，其在 2026 年已拦截 57 起疑似与朝鲜相关的威胁，本次攻击是漏网的一起。

Remediation Binance says is done as of Nov 3: wallet system rebuilt in a new environment; internal AI tool and its access controls rebuilt; affected devices replaced and credentials retired; enhanced scans across all employee devices; strengthened AI-based anomaly detection and wallet risk controls; independent cybersecurity review being commissioned. Still not published: the internal AI tool's permissions model — how it could be used to reach other engineers' machines. / 币安称截至 11 月 3 日已完成的整改：在新环境中重建钱包系统；重建内部 AI 工具及其访问控制；更换受影响设备并作废相关凭证；对所有员工设备加强扫描；强化基于 AI 的异常检测与钱包风控；正在委托独立网络安全审查。仍未公开：内部 AI 工具的权限模型——它为何能被用来触达其他工程师的机器。

4. Who is blamed 归因

Partly resolved. Binance's Nov 3 report cites the FBI attributing the attack to North Korea (DPRK). Secondary coverage of FBI/TRM Labs/Chainalysis reporting names the specific unit as TraderTraitor, the North Korean state-sponsored group the FBI also named for the $1.5B Bybit theft (Feb 2025), with high confidence. North Korea has denied the attribution (per secondary reporting). TraderTraitor's known playbook targets people rather than technology — fake job offers and interviews (“Contagious Interview”) that trick developers into running malware on their own machines — which matches the September stage Binance describes. The preliminary page's open question “will Binance confirm TraderTraitor?” is therefore answered in substance on the country attribution (DPRK, via the FBI), with the unit name coming from FBI/industry reporting rather than from Binance's own text naming it.

部分解决。币安 11 月 3 日报告援引 FBI 将攻击归因于朝鲜。关于 FBI/TRM Labs/Chainalysis 报道的二手报道将具体组织点名为 TraderTraitor——即 FBI 认定的 2025 年 2 月 Bybit 15 亿美元盗窃案实施者，并称高度确信。朝鲜已否认该归因（据二手报道）。TraderTraitor 的惯用手法是攻击人而非技术——以虚假招聘与面试（“Contagious Interview”）诱骗开发者在自己的电脑上运行恶意代码——这与币安描述的 9 月阶段吻合。因此，本页此前“币安是否会确认 TraderTraitor”的待确认问题，在国家归因层面已基本得到回答（朝鲜，据 FBI），具体组织名称来自 FBI/行业报道，而非币安原文直接点名。

5. Was AI involved? AI 是否参与？

Verdict — REVISED (Nov 3): Attackers abused AI tooling and the access it carried. Binance now confirms a tampered AI coding-tool installer was the June entry point and an internal AI tool was abused as the Oct 20 pivot. Still not evidenced: an autonomous AI agent carrying out the theft. The accurate line is “attackers abused AI tools,” not “AI hacked Binance.” This supersedes the preliminary page's “AI evidenced only on defense” conclusion. / 结论——已修正（11 月 3 日）：攻击者滥用了 AI 工具及其携带的权限。币安现已确认：被篡改的 AI 编程工具安装包是 6 月的入口，内部 AI 工具在 10 月 20 日被滥用为横向渗透的跳板。仍然没有证据表明自主 AI 智能体实施了盗窃。准确的表述是“攻击者滥用了 AI 工具”，而非“AI 黑了币安”。本结论取代本页初步版本中“AI 仅在防御方有证据”的说法。

Why the stronger AI story spread: Elon Musk, reacting to a viral post about the attacker dodging stablecoin freezes by borrowing ETH against stolen USDT on Aave, posted "Everyday is Zeroday" and wrote that "super intelligence agents will at some point do this to gain currency for themselves, not a human." That is speculation about the future, not a claim about this incident. A second viral version — "an AI agent scanned millions of files in three seconds, found an engineer's password, and emptied the vault" — remains contradicted by Binance's account (no password or private key was taken; the signing system was misused through prepared authorizations). AI also played a verified role on defense: Blockaid says its AI models flagged the drain within about two minutes of the first sweep, and Binance says it has since strengthened AI-based anomaly detection. Elliptic (Nov 6) likewise notes the laundering looks highly automated but provides no evidence of direct AI involvement in the laundering.

更强的 AI 叙事为何传播：Elon Musk 在回应一条关于攻击者用被盗 USDT 在 Aave 抵押借出 ETH 以绕开冻结的病毒帖时，发帖称“Everyday is Zeroday”，并写道“超级智能体将来总有一天会这样做，为自己获取资金，而不是为人类”。这是对未来的推测，而非对本次事件的断言。另一个病毒版本称“AI 智能体在三秒内扫描了数百万文件、找到工程师密码并掏空金库”——这仍与币安的说法直接矛盾（没有密码或私钥被窃取；签名系统是经预先准备的授权被滥用的）。AI 在防御方也确有经证实的角色：Blockaid 表示其 AI 模型在第一波转出后约两分钟内就标记了异常，币安称此后已强化基于 AI 的异常检测。Elliptic（11 月 6 日）同样指出洗钱过程看起来高度自动化，但没有提供 AI 直接参与洗钱的证据。

The nuance that now matters most: in 2026 an engineer's laptop holds tokens, cloud sessions, repository access — and AI tooling that acts with that engineer's permissions. Binance's own account confirms that tooling and its access can become the pivot, which is why endpoint and AI-tool permission hygiene matters more now. That is still different from an autonomous agent deciding to steal. / 如今最值得保留的细微点：2026 年的工程师笔记本里装着令牌、云会话、代码仓库权限——以及以该工程师权限行事的 AI 工具。币安自己的报告确认，这类工具及其权限可能成为攻击跳板，这正是终端与 AI 工具权限卫生如今更重要的原因。但这仍不同于自主智能体自行决定实施盗窃。

  • Binance's full technical post-mortem — STILL OPEN, now the main remaining item, together with the independent cybersecurity review Binance says it is commissioning. Binance says some technical details will remain confidential, so a fully public technical account may never arrive. / 币安完整技术复盘——仍未解决，现为最主要的待确认事项，以及币安称正在委托的独立网络安全审查。币安表示部分技术细节将保密，因此完全公开的技术说明可能永远不会出现。
  • Final confirmed (reconciled) loss figure: currently ~$6.34B affected and ~$2.85B frozen/intercepted per Binance (Nov 3), subject to reconciliation and price moves; Elliptic's Nov 6 accounting uses $6.43B. / 最终确认（对账后）的损失数字：目前按币安 11 月 3 日为受影响约 63.4 亿美元、冻结/拦截约 28.5 亿美元，待对账且受价格波动影响；Elliptic 11 月 6 日的统计使用 64.3 亿美元。
