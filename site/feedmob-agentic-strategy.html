Feedmob's Position: The Open-Web Play in Agentic Advertising

Why a mobile UA company sitting on diversified third-party inventory holds good cards in both Family A and Family C — and the five moves to make.

Feedmob 的位置：开放网络在智能广告时代的机会

为什么手握多样化第三方流量的移动 UA 公司，在 Family A 与 Family C 都有好牌可打——以及应该采取的五个行动。

TL;DR

The walled gardens are automating inside their own walls — but they can't offer cross-publisher reach with transparent, post-install measurement. Feedmob's diversified inventory (OEMs, indie publishers, networks) is exactly what they can't offer. Meanwhile, when AI agents become the shoppers, discovery happens across the open ecosystem — Feedmob's network is that surface, and performance UA survives the transition because its KPI is a verifiable post-install event, not an impression that may never meet a human eye.

Bottom line: build the "Advantage+ for the open mobile web," treat cross-publisher data as the moat, productize anti-fraud, experiment in Family C while land is cheap — and never become a dumb pipe.

核心结论

围墙花园只在自家墙内做自动化——它们给不了广告主跨渠道的覆盖，更给不了透明、可验证的安装后效果衡量。Feedmob 的多元化流量（OEM、独立开发者、广告网络）正是它们给不了的东西。而当 AI 成为购物者，应用的"被发现"发生在开放生态中——Feedmob 的流量网络正是这层渠道；效果类 UA 比品牌广告更能穿越这个转变，因为它的 KPI 是可验证的安装后事件，而不是可能永远见不到人眼的一次"曝光"。

一句话：打造"开放移动网络的 Advantage+"，把跨渠道数据当护城河，把反作弊做成产品，趁地价便宜先试 Family C——绝不当"哑管道"。

Family A  Why we're positioned: AI doing the work

Meta's Advantage+, Google's AI Max, Amazon's Ads Agent — the biggest "real budget" story in agentic advertising — all share one property: they only optimize inside their own walls. Meta's AI will never buy Samsung inventory. Google's will never optimize across indie publishers.

That creates the exact pain advertisers already feel: three black boxes, rising CPMs, and attribution that can't be independently verified — worse since ATT. What the gardens structurally cannot sell is cross-publisher reach with transparent, post-install-KPI-driven buying. That is precisely Feedmob's inventory and operating model.

As AI takes over buying within each garden, the scarce value moves to whoever aggregates and optimizes across them. There is no "Advantage+ for the open mobile web" yet. That gap is the opening.

Update · Oct 7, 2026  InMobi's Mobius now claims this slot — see Competitor watch below. The gap is contested, not empty; our counter is the independent layer.

Family A  为什么我们有位置：AI 干活

Meta Advantage+、Google AI Max、Amazon Ads Agent——智能广告里"真金白银"最大的故事——都有一个共同点：它们只在自家墙内优化。Meta 的 AI 永远不会去买 Samsung 的流量，Google 的 AI 也不会去跨独立开发者做优化。

这正是广告主已经在忍受的痛：三个黑盒、CPM 上涨、归因无法独立验证——ATT 之后更甚。而围墙花园在结构上就卖不了的东西，是跨渠道覆盖 + 透明的、按安装后 KPI 结算的买量。这恰恰是 Feedmob 的流量和运营模式。

当 AI 接管了每个花园内部的买量，稀缺价值就转移到能跨花园聚合和优化的人手里。"开放移动网络的 Advantage+" 还不存在。这个空位就是机会。

更新 · 2026年10月7日  InMobi 的 Mobius 已经宣称占据了这个位置——见下文竞争者动态。空位不再是空的，而是有对手了；我们的反击是独立的买量层。

Family C  Why we're positioned: AI as the customer

When agents research and buy on a consumer's behalf, advertising changes from catching human eyeballs to being chosen by the agent. Agents discover apps across the open ecosystem — not inside a single walled garden. Feedmob's network is the surface where that discovery happens.

And here's the underappreciated part: performance UA survives the Family C transition better than brand advertising. A brand "impression" may never meet a human eye — but a post-install event is produced by the agent's own verifiable action. The KPI doesn't break when the customer becomes software.

Early is another word for cheap land. Only 8% of brands have an AI-answer strategy today; the companies that build for agent-customers now own the position when the behavior arrives.

Family C  为什么我们有位置：AI 作为消费者

当 AI 替用户做研究、下单，广告就从"抓住人眼"变成"被 agent 选中"。Agent 在开放生态里发现应用——不在某一家围墙花园里。Feedmob 的流量网络，正是这层"被发现"发生的渠道。

还有一个被低估的点：效果类 UA 比品牌广告更能穿越这个转变。品牌的一次"曝光"可能永远见不到人眼，但一次安装后事件，是 agent 自己行为的可验证产物——客户变成软件，KPI 不会失效。

"早期"是"地价便宜"的另一种说法。今天只有 8% 的品牌有 AI 问答策略；现在就为 agent 消费者做建设的公司，等行为到来时就已经占住了位置。

Competitor watch: InMobi's Mobius Oct 5, 2026

One day before this note was written, InMobi announced Mobius, a "unified intelligence system for advertising decisioning" spanning its Exchange and DSP plus owned surfaces (Glance, 1Weather). The pitch: evaluate every eligible option against the advertiser's stated outcome, select what runs, and record the basis for each choice so the next decision starts from evidence. Claimed scale: 25 trillion predictions a day, 200 billion ad opportunities, drawing on 70,000+ SDK-integrated publisher apps.

What it means: this validates the thesis above — a scaled mobile-ad player is now claiming the "open-web AI buying layer" slot. The gap this note framed is no longer empty; it's contested.

The counter-position it leaves open: Mobius is vertically integrated — InMobi sells the intelligence, the inventory, and the owned surfaces. The open counter is the independent layer that isn't grading its own homework. Their "decision trace" bar is the right one; our answer is independent measurement (action 5) and cross-publisher post-install KPIs (action 2).

Family C too: Glance is positioned as agentic commerce — agents discovering and shopping. Our "agent discoverability" bets (action 4) are aimed at the same land.

What changes: action 1's clock speeds up. The winning position isn't being first to sell "an AI buying layer" — it's being the independent alternative to vertically integrated giants. Keep building; don't copy Mobius's shape, counter it.

竞争者动态：InMobi Mobius 2026年10月5日

就在这篇备忘写成的前一天，InMobi 发布了 Mobius——"广告决策的统一智能系统"，覆盖它的 Exchange、DSP 和自有渠道（Glance、1Weather）。玩法：按广告主设定的目标评估所有可选方案，决定投放什么，并记录每次决策的依据，让下一次决策从证据开始。声称的规模：每天 25 万亿次预测、2000 亿次广告机会，数据源包括 7 万+ 接入 SDK 的应用。

含义：这验证了上面的判断——有规模的移动广告玩家已经开始占领"开放网络的 AI 买量层"这个位置。这篇备忘里说的"空位"不再是空的，而是有对手了。

它留出的反击位置：Mobius 是垂直整合——InMobi 同时卖智能、卖流量、卖自有渠道。开放的反击是独立的、透明的买量层——不自己给自己判分。他们打出的"决策可追溯"是正确的标准；我们的答案是独立衡量（行动 5）和跨渠道的安装后 KPI（行动 2）。

Family C 也是他们的方向：Glance 被定位为 agent 商业——agent 发现、agent 购物。我们的"agent 可发现性"下注（行动 4）打的是同一块地。

变化：行动 1 的时钟加快了。胜出的位置不是第一个卖"AI 买量层"，而是垂直整合巨头们的独立替代品。继续建；不要复制 Mobius 的形状，要反制它。

Expectation: where this goes

Now → 2027

The gardens keep automating inward. Open-web UA players that add their own AI optimization layer take share from manual media buying. "Getting recommended by AI" moves from 73%-monitoring to real strategy budgets.

Medium term

Agent-to-agent buying protocols (AdCP / AAMP) settle. Whoever holds aggregated supply + trusted measurement becomes the sell-side rail — the indispensable intermediary agents transact through.

The risk

Sit still and you become dumb pipes: someone else's agent does the thinking, your inventory gets bought at declining margins. The platforms' AI will happily commoditize any supply it can reach.

预期：事情会往哪里去

现在 → 2027

围墙花园继续向内自动化。有自家 AI 优化层的开放网络 UA 玩家，从人工买量手里抢份额。"被 AI 推荐"从 73% 的观望变成真正的策略预算。

中期

Agent 间买量协议（AdCP / AAMP）尘埃落定。手握聚合供给 + 可信衡量的公司成为卖方通道——agent 交易必经的中间层。

风险

原地不动就会变成哑管道：别人的 agent 负责思考，你的流量以越来越低的毛利被买走。平台的 AI 很乐意把它们能触达的一切供给商品化。

Five actions

1. Build the AI buying layer for open inventory

Automated media mix, creative versioning (the promo-video capability applies directly), post-install optimization — pitched as the transparent alternative to black-box garden automation.

2. Treat cross-publisher KPI data as the moat

Post-install performance data across 500+ media sources is training data the walled gardens don't have. That's what makes action 1 defensible instead of copyable.

3. Productize anti-fraud

Agent traffic creates brand-new fraud surfaces — agent clicks, agent-driven fake installs. Anti-fraud goes from cost center to selling point; make it a standalone offering.

4. Experiment in Family C now, cheaply

An "agent discoverability" package: getting clients' apps recommended by shopping assistants, testing sponsored placements inside AI assistants (that's Family B — real budgets too). Small bets while land is cheap.

5. Own independent measurement

Already SKAN-certified — extend the story to agent-attribution. When the industry's test question comes — "who's doing it today, with how much money?" — have the answer ready.

五个行动

1. 为开放流量打造 AI 买量层

自动化的媒介组合、素材批量生产（你们的 promo 视频能力直接用得上）、安装后优化——打法是"透明"，对标围墙花园的黑盒。

2. 把跨渠道 KPI 数据当护城河

500+ 媒体来源的安装后效果数据，是围墙花园没有的训练数据。这才是行动 1 能成立、且别人抄不走的原因。

3. 把反作弊做成产品

Agent 流量会带来全新的作弊形态——agent 点击、agent 驱动的假安装。反作弊从成本中心变成卖点，做成独立产品。

4. 现在就小步试 Family C

"Agent 可发现性"套餐：帮客户的应用被购物助手推荐、在 AI 助手里测试赞助位（这也是 Family B，同样有真预算）。趁地价便宜先占坑。

5. 掌握独立衡量的定义权

已经是 SKAN 认证，下一步讲 agent 归因的故事。当行业开始问那个问题——"今天谁在做？花了多少钱？"——我们要有答案。

The question to keep asking, per the article's test: for every agentic bet — who is doing it today, with how much money, and what would have to be true for it to grow beyond a test budget? Feedmob's answers: open-inventory AI buying (pilots now, Family A budgets exist), agent discoverability (early, land is cheap), independent measurement (the unlock for everything else).
按文章里的标准持续追问：每一个 agentic 下注——今天谁在做？花了多少钱？要怎样才能长出测试预算之外的规模？Feedmob 的答案：开放流量的 AI 买量（现在试点，Family A 的真预算存在）、agent 可发现性（早期，地价便宜）、独立衡量（其他一切的钥匙）。
